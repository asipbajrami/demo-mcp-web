Mjedoso

Integrated Environmental Management System

About the System

Mjedoso is a comprehensive digital platform for the National Environmental Agency of Albania that streamlines environmental permits, monitoring, and reporting processes.

Key Modules

Environmental Permit Management

Process Type A and B permits online

Self-Monitoring & Reporting

Electronic submission and verification

Waste Management Registry

Track all waste types and licenses

Environmental Impact Assessment

Digital EIA submission and review

System Integrations

  • e-Albania Portal
  • National Business Center (QKB)
  • e-Inspection System
  • Civil Registry
  • Address Registry
